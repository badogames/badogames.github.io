ColorMellow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game ColorMellow (package com.colormellow.game, "the app") handles information. ColorMellow is a color by number game. You can play it without creating an account and without giving us any personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game data only on your device: coloring progress, finished pictures, coins, levels, achievements, daily rewards, settings and any puzzles you create from photos. We do not receive or store this data on any server. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Photos

When you choose "Create from photo", the app reads only the single image you select, converts it into a puzzle on your device, and saves the resulting puzzle locally. Your photos are never uploaded or shared. If you choose "Save image" or "Share", the finished artwork is saved to your device so you can share it yourself.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ColorMellow is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as model, OS version and app version
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and taps, plus diagnostics and crash information

Google uses this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information. We do not receive any data that directly identifies you.

Your choices: In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's Settings → Google → Ads. Watching rewarded videos in the app is always optional.

Children

ColorMellow is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: needed to load ads.
  • Vibration: optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is transmitted encrypted and kept according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
